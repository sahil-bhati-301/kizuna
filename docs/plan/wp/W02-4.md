## Goal
Load a subset of Japanese Tatoeba sentences with their English translations, each linked to the JMdict words it contains, for context drills and example sentences.

## Acceptance criteria (each must be testable)
1. The importer reads the Japanese sentences, the links file and the English sentences, and builds `sentence` rows with the Tatoeba ID, Japanese text (NFC) and lang-tagged translations of directly linked sentences.
2. Word links come from the Japanese word index (`jpn_indices`, B-line): each indexed headword, with its optional reading, maps to a loaded `word.ent_seq`; an ambiguous or unknown mapping is counted in the report, never guessed.
3. The subset follows Q1; `quality` follows Q2; `word_ids` are sorted and unique.
4. Every row carries `source`, `source_version`, `license` (CC BY 2.0 FR) and enough to link the sentence on tatoeba.org (per-sentence attribution); the attribution URL format is tested.
5. The same inputs give byte-identical output (golden file from a fixture of about 10 sentences, including one with no English translation and one with an unmapped index word); the Tatoeba files are pinned in the lock and the full-run counts are reported.

## Out of scope
- Our own tokeniser (we use Tatoeba's index). Sentence audio. CC0 licence detection per sentence (backlog if needed). Languages other than English. DB load (W03-5).
